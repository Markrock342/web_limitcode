## Changes chosen from this review

1. Add `/guides/erp-import-distribution`: an original walkthrough based on our own demo document chain, with two screenshots captured from our sample application. Identify the content as a demo, not a live-client case study.
2. Explain the sales-order → purchasing → shipment → landed-cost references, so buyers can evaluate a process rather than only a dashboard screenshot.
3. Add a packaged/custom comparison and a cost-component checklist to the ERP service page. Use questions to verify, not unsupported claims that one option is always cheaper or faster.
4. Publish a Thai UTF-8 CSV planning checklist, downloadable without a form or account. It is a planning aid, not a data importer or production ERP integration.
5. Link the walkthrough from the homepage service teaser, ERP service and sitemap. Give it a self canonical, an accurate publication date and factual Article/BreadcrumbList data.

## Work that needs evidence or owner decisions

- Public project prices, guaranteed delivery dates, support terms and source-code rights need a defined offer. None were inferred from competitors' prices or private customer quotations.
- Client case studies require permission and verified facts. Screenshots here use fictional demo data only.
- Independent mentions and backlinks require relevant relationships and legitimate editorial reasons. No links were purchased, no messages were sent to vendors/customers, and no reviews were fabricated.
- Search Console impressions, query positions and customer-inquiry metrics are private operational data. Do not commit them to this public repository. Record future comparisons privately, using the same query/date/country/device filters.

Indexing requests and IndexNow receipt are discovery steps. They do not demonstrate Top 10 placement or AI citation. Measure each target query over time rather than counting brand searches as success for ERP service queries.
